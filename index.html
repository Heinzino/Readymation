FAQ

Questions we get on every call.

What counts as a "qualified appointment"?
We agree on the criteria together on the strategy call — typically company type, size, and the decision-maker's role. Our team confirms each prospect fits and wants to talk before it goes on your calendar. If it doesn't meet the criteria, you don't pay for it.
What happens if the prospect doesn't show up?
You don't pay. Simple as that. We send reminders and reschedule no-shows on our end, and you're only billed for appointments where the prospect actually attends.
How fast will I see appointments?
Infrastructure and list building take the first couple of weeks. Once sending starts, bookings typically begin within days — we've had clients get their first two calls within hours of launch.
Who is this a good fit for?
B2B companies that sell over a call and have a clear ideal customer — recruiting and staffing firms, payment processing, agencies, consultancies, and professional services. If your deal size can justify paying per appointment, it's usually a fit. We'll tell you honestly on the call if it isn't.
Will this hurt my domain reputation?
No. All outreach runs on separate sending domains and mailboxes that we set up and warm up. Your primary domain is never used for cold email.
What do I need to provide?
About an hour of your time for the strategy call, a clear picture of who you want to talk to, your calendar availability, and the infrastructure and list costs — which you own outright. We handle the rest.
